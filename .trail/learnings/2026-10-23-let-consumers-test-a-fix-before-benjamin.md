## Decided
For a fix or new feature that consumers depend on, give them a test build first. Use a yalc build or a beta tarball with a checksum, and post it on the bridge thread. Wait for their reply before cutting the release.
This applies to patch releases as well as the 1.0 launch. The user wants contributors to test before anything is final.
